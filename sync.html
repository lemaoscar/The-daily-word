YOU ARE IN CONTROL

How sync works

  • Sign-in uses an email link. Display name and profile photo are optional.
  • Your data is encrypted in this browser; the passphrase never leaves it.
  • Sync is manual. On conflicts, notes and saved lists are merged conservatively.
  • Local device data remains available if you sign out or lose internet.
  • Keep an encrypted file backup too. Forgetting the passphrase means the cloud copy cannot be recovered.
